PhD candidate in finance at Alliance Manchester Business School. I work on
return and volatility forecasting, and I write the R that goes with it.

What is public here is research code: packages that came out of my own
projects, replications of papers I needed to understand before trusting, and
pipelines for the data everyone in this field rebuilds from scratch.

## A few things here

**[forecastdom](https://github.com/gabbocg/forecastdom)** — conditional and unconditional forecast dominance testing.

**[sdim](https://github.com/gabbocg/sdim)** — supervised dimension reduction.

**[pead](https://github.com/gabbocg/pead)** — standardized earnings surprises from WRDS, end to end.

**[qkit](https://github.com/gabbocg/qkit)** / **[uomthesis](https://github.com/gabbocg/uomthesis)** — the Quarto templates I write everything in, thesis included.

**[py4fin](https://github.com/gabbocg/py4fin)** — Introduction to Python for Finance (ENGIN604), course materials.

Repositories named after a journal and a year (`IRFA-2024`, `JCOMM-2023`,
`JRPO-2021`) hold the code behind published papers.
